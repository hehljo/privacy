Henga contains no analytics or tracking libraries, no third-party crash reporting, no advertising networks and no cookies. No usage behaviour is recorded.

Your information is processed in order to perform the usage relationship and to provide the functionality you requested, pursuant to Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. As no data reaches the provider, no processing by the provider takes place in this respect.

Your information remains stored until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Stored credentials can additionally be reviewed and removed at any time in your operating system's keychain.
